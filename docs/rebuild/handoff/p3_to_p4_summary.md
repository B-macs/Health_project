# Phase 3 → Phase 4 handoff (2026-10-20)

**Done: the design.** The owner chose: *"I pick week for everything but training, where I pick cards for training"*. Every p3 counter is 0; signed off under his standing instruction.

## The design (docs/rebuild/design.md)
- The colour rule: one colour family per page — Today teal, Training amber, Insights indigo, Check-in and Settings slate. A status is a word.
- Week's frame: a glow at the top of each page, glass cards, thin numbers, a glass tab bar with five tabs.
- Today: a week strip with a small readiness ring per day, the readiness tile with its sentence, sleep and strain tiles, the plan as a timeline with the Start button.
- Training (Cards): the exercise card with its set tracker, large timer digits over a bar, the controls, the next set; no tab bar; the screen stays on.
- Insights: two number tiles, the 12-week area chart, the body areas as contributor bars.
- Screenshots: `docs/rebuild/handoff/p3_screens/`.
