## Unranked source advice

From leg-workout videos that rank nothing (pasted 2026-10-15):

- One straight-knee and one bent-knee calf exercise (Jeff Nippard, leg day;
  Built With Science, leg workout).
- Jeff Nippard notes studies where the soleus — the bent-knee target — grows
  poorly, so straight-knee work may be the better use of the sets; he keeps both
  until a direct comparison exists.
- Toe direction: Jeff Nippard (leg day 1) cites a 2020 study where toes out
  grew the inner calf more and toes in the outer calf.
