# Workout videos — recommendations WITHOUT tiers

Three of the five videos the athlete pasted on 2026-10-15 are leg WORKOUTS, not
tier lists. They recommend exercises and give reasons, but they rank nothing,
so no tier is invented for them here. Each area below lists what the sources
recommend; an area gets tiers only from a source that ranks.

- **Video 1** — Jeff Nippard, push-pull-legs leg day (the transcript names his
  site). Deadlift top set, stiff-leg deadlift, leg press, glute-ham raise,
  slow-lowering leg extension, seated calf raise, Roman chair leg raise.
- **Video 4** — Built With Science, leg workout (the transcript names its site).
  Back or front squat, barbell hip thrust, split squat progressing to the
  Bulgarian split squat, glute-ham raise, standing and seated calf raises.
- **Video 5** — Jeff Nippard, push-pull-legs leg day 1. Squat top set with paused
  back-off sets, barbell Romanian deadlift, dumbbell walking lunge, seated leg
  curl, leg-press calf raise, decline plate crunch.

## By area

**Hamstrings** (no tier list yet):
- Seated leg curl — video 5 cites a 2021 study with clearly more hamstring
  growth than the lying leg curl (the same study as Maeo et al. 2021, checked
  2026-10-15).
- Glute-ham raise — videos 1 and 4; video 4 cites an EMG analysis with the
  highest hamstring activity among common hamstring exercises. Video 1 trims the
  top quarter, where the hamstrings carry no tension.
- Romanian deadlift — video 5: for the hamstrings, cut the top 10-25% of the
  lift and pause at the bottom.
- Stiff-leg deadlift (video 1) and Nordic curl (video 1, as an alternative).

**Quads:** leg press for volume, with a slight knee bend at the top (video 1);
slow-lowering leg extension for the rectus femoris, which squats and leg presses
do not train fully (video 1); the front squat puts less load on the knees and
the lower back than the back squat (video 4).

**Glutes:** hip thrust as the complement to the squat (video 4, which also notes
a 12-week study where squats grew the glutes about twice as much as hip
thrusts); the split squat progressing to the Bulgarian split squat, then a
dumbbell in the opposite hand for more glute medius (video 4); the dumbbell
walking lunge at full depth with a controlled lowering (video 5).

**Calves** (not one of his areas): one straight-knee and one bent-knee calf
exercise (videos 1 and 4); video 1 notes studies where the soleus, the
bent-knee target, grows poorly; video 5 cites a 2020 study where toes out grew
the inner calf and toes in the outer calf.

**Abs:** Roman chair leg raise (video 1) — the leg-raise family is out for him
until gate 1 passes; decline plate crunch (video 5) — not in the abs tier list.
