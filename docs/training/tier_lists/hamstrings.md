## Unranked source advice

From leg-workout videos that rank nothing (pasted 2026-10-15):

- **Seated leg curl** — Jeff Nippard (leg day 1) cites a 2021 study with clearly
  more hamstring growth than the lying leg curl, because the seated position
  trains the hamstrings longer. This is Maeo et al. 2021, Med Sci Sports Exerc
  (checked 2026-10-15: 20 people, 12 weeks, more growth in the hamstring heads
  that cross the hip). Set the seat so the hamstrings feel stretched at the
  start.
- **Glute-ham raise** — Jeff Nippard (leg day) and Built With Science (leg
  workout). Built With Science cites an EMG analysis with the highest hamstring
  activity among common hamstring exercises; Jeff Nippard leaves out the top
  quarter, where the hamstrings carry no tension.
- **Romanian deadlift** — Jeff Nippard (leg day 1): for the hamstrings, cut the
  top 10-25% of the lift and pause at the bottom; keep the weight close to the
  legs.
- **Stiff-leg deadlift** and **Nordic curl** (as the alternative to the
  glute-ham raise) — Jeff Nippard (leg day).
