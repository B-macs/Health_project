# Deep back muscles

**No ranked list yet** for the deep back muscles as their own group. Two ranked
lists rank back-extension exercises for other reasons; their rows are copied
below, so that step 5 reads them in this area's file.

The area is goal A priority 1 (step 3: at least 10 hard sets a week, on 2 or
more days), job "strength and size" (weak list, assessment 2026-06-28).

## Ranked in other areas' lists

| Exercise | List | Tier | Source's main reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hyperextension and reverse hyperextension (ranked together) | Hams and glutes — [hamstrings.md](hamstrings.md) #5 | A | Good to build the lower back, or for rehab after a back injury |
| 45° back extension, upper back rounded | Glutes, Jeff Nippard — [glutes.md](glutes.md) #23 | **S** | Ranked for the GLUTES: they work at the bottom and at the top |

The 45° back extension is one form of the hyperextension: the bench is set at
45°. The rounded upper back in the glute list moves the work toward the glutes.

## Against the athlete's rules (checked 2026-10-15)

- **Safety rules:** "back extension", "hyperextension" and "reverse
  hyperextension" all meet the lumbar-extension rule at L5/S1: "caution",
  allowed (downgraded from "contraindicated" on 2026-08-17, his decision). The
  movement stops at a straight line, never past it.
- **The lower back is not bent under load:** the safety rules contraindicate
  loaded bending of the lower back (the covered annulus tears at L3/4 and L4/5
  on the MRI). In these exercises the hips move and the back stays straight.
- **Gym:** the 45° back extension bench is confirmed (2026-10-15). A reverse
  hyperextension machine is not confirmed.

**Pick — APPROVED 2026-10-16** (*"yes agreed"*): the 45° back extension, back
straight, up to a straight line. It counts 1 for the deep back muscles and 0.5
for glute max.

## Unranked source advice

- **Built With Science — glute EMG test** (video 5 of 6, 2026-10-15): it refers
  to the channel's back test, where deadlifts worked the whole back well, and
  found deadlifts good for the lower glutes too — one exercise for the whole
  back of the body.
- **Built With Science — "five leg mistakes"** (video 6 of 6, 2026-10-15): it
  cites EMG work where the lower-back muscles work almost as hard as some quad
  muscles in a barbell back squat. Barbells are out for him, and the back
  support of a hack squat takes most of that work away — so the machine squat
  gives the deep back muscles little, and this area needs its own exercise.
