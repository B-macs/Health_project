## Against the athlete's rules (checked 2026-10-16)

- **The measured right-arm limit** (a face-down arm raise: 20 cm on the right
  against 40 cm on the left, 2026-08-13 — the Latarjet limits the arm behind
  the body): the chest-supported row with the elbows wide and the face pull end
  with the arm behind the body, so the right side stops early and the left does
  more — the opposite of this slot's job. Out for this slot.
- **Pec minor** helps pull the shoulder blades down and forward, and it is the
  tight muscle at the front of the right shoulder (goal B). The scapular
  pulldowns load it. Out for this slot.
- **The hang** (scapular pull-up): the shoulder's hang ladder is held at its
  second step; it carries the most load on the shoulder in the A tier.
- **What is left in the A tier:** the single-arm cable Y raise — tension with
  the arm across the body, load in steps of half a unit (about 0.25 kg; every
  cable since 2026-10-22), one arm at a
  time (his single-arm rule) — and the wall slide, which carries almost no load.
- **The prone Y raise (B)** is hardest at the top, where the lower traps are
  shortest, and his lightest dumbbells go from 2.5 to 5 kg, a 100% step.
- **Safety rules:** the cable Y raise reads "unknown" and gets a rule before use.

**Check of the pick (step 8, 2026-10-16): the single-arm cable Y raise (A)
ranks above the prone Y raise (B)** for the same job. **APPROVED 2026-10-16**
(*"1 yes"*): it replaces the prone Y raise. One arm at a time costs about 3
minutes more than the prone Y raise on each press day — a cost not stated when
he was asked, and given to him afterwards.
