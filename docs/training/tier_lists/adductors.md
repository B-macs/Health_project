## Against the athlete's rules (checked 2026-10-16)

- **The ladder's rungs** rank: the ball squeeze (B), the side-lying lift (B), the
  short-lever Copenhagen plank, held (C), and the long-lever Copenhagen (A).
  Copenhagen work raises adductor strength in trials (Ishøi et al. 2016; Harøy
  et al. 2017).
- **Gym:** the adductor machine is not on the confirmed list — asked.
- **Lower safety margin:** the frog lift-offs and the Cossack squat bend the hip
  and turn it out, the right hip's click trigger (finding #4), at end range with
  Beighton 6/9. The cable adduction keeps the knee straight, not the tested
  knees-bent position.
- **Already in the week:** the Sunday session trains the range in the tested
  position (tailor's pose, frog rocks, butterfly contract-relax).
- **Safety rules:** the adductor machine reads "unknown" and gets a rule before
  use.

**Check of the pick (step 8, 2026-10-16): the adductor machine (S) ranks above
the ladder, if his gym has one** — knees bent, starting wide under load, small
steps on the stack: strength at length in the tested position. It moves the
adductor work from home into the gym, about 5 minutes on two sessions. Without
one, the ladder stays: no A exercise has a better safety margin, and its top
rung has trial evidence. **His decision is asked.**
