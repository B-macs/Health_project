# Back (lats and upper back) — the decider's tier list

**No source has ranked back exercises yet.** This list is the tier decider's
(`services/tier_decider.py`; the ratings and their rules are in
`exercise_features.py`), made 2026-10-16 at the athlete's request: *"use the
same script to create a tier list for back and chest, I can copy in later
videos to see if you're correct but go for that for now."*

- **How close it gets:** on the five lists already recorded, scored on a list it
  had not been calibrated on, it gives the source's exact tier 46 times in 111
  (41%) and comes within one tier 107 times (96%). Guessing "A" for everything
  gives 32% and 74%. Expect about four exact tiers in ten, and most of the rest
  one tier off.
- **Used like a source list until a video arrives** (athlete: *"go for that for
  now"*): step 5 picks from its S and A tiers. When a video for this area is
  pasted, its list replaces this one, and
  `python scripts/tier_decider.py compare <file>` records how close the decider
  was.
- **Known weak spot:** the decider sees risk only through "comfort", so a
  position most lists mark down for joint risk scores higher than a video
  would likely put it (here, the behind-the-neck pulldown). The athlete's own rules remove those anyway.
- **The method:** score = 3 × stretch + target + stable + load + comfort,
  minus 4 for a combination move, jump, swing or cardio (−4 to 14). D from 3,
  C from 4, B from 6, A from 8, S from 13 — cut points calibrated on the five
  recorded lists.

## Against the athlete's rules (checked 2026-10-16)

- **Barbells are out:** the barbell bent-over row, the Pendlay row, the Yates
  row, the conventional deadlift and the rack pull.
- **Out by his own precaution** (2026-10-16): the behind-the-neck lat pulldown.
- **His single-arm rule** (goal B, step 2: single-arm work where possible, so
  that the left cannot do the right's work) favours the single-arm dumbbell row,
  the single-arm cable pulldown and the single-arm cable row.
- **The measured right-arm limit:** a row takes the elbow behind the body at
  the top; on the right it goes as far as it goes easily (a face-down arm raise,
  20 cm right against 40 cm left, 2026-08-13).
- **Gym:** lat pulldown, cable stack, dumbbells and benches are confirmed. Not
  confirmed: the assisted pull-up machine, the chest-supported T-bar row, the
  machine row, the high row machine and the machine pullover.
- **Safety rules:** every back exercise here reads "unknown"; the pick gets a
  rule before it is used.

**Pick (step 5, 2026-10-16, for his approval):** the **single-arm dumbbell row**
(S). Several S rows tie on tier, safety and directness; his single-arm rule and
known tolerance (it ran in Block B) break the tie. One back exercise of 3 sets
in two sessions a week gives the area its 6 hard sets.
