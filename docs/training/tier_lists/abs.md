# Abs — tier list

- **Source:** Built With Science (YouTube), *"It's Weird, But Destroyed Every Ab
  Exercise I Tested"* — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ShG8aVIvms
- **Recorded:** 2026-10-15, from the transcript the athlete pasted. Reasons are
  short paraphrases, not quotes.
- **Scale:** S (best) → A → B → C → D → F (fail).
- **The source's three criteria for a good ab exercise:**
  1. Load can be added over time.
  2. The abs are under tension in a long, stretched position.
  3. The hip flexors do not do most of the work.
- **What it measured:** surface EMG on the upper abs, lower abs and hip flexors,
  on two people — an experienced lifter and a beginner.
- **What it did not measure:** muscle growth. The tiers are the presenter's
  judgement from the EMG, the three criteria and how each exercise felt. The
  source itself says no study has tested whether upper- or lower-ab exercises
  grow those parts more.
- **Coverage:** the front wall of the abs only. There are NO side (oblique)
  exercises in this list.

## The source's final picks

- **Beginner:** #30 face-away lat-pulldown cable crunch (upper) + #19 reverse
  crunch on an ab mat (lower).
- **Advanced:** #30 face-away lat-pulldown cable crunch (upper) + #17 Garhammer
  raise (lower).

## Against the athlete's rules (checked 2026-10-15)

- **Safety rules:** every crunch variant (#1-5, #18-19, #21, #26-30) is
  "caution" — loaded bending of the spine, downgraded from contraindicated by
  the athlete's decision of 2026-08-17. The hanging exercises (#7-10) fall under
  the hang rule (caution, both arms always). Most of the rest are "unknown".
  #23 matches the soles-together hip stretch rule by mistake (a name collision).
- **His decision of 2026-10-05** — no leg-raise work, because it pulls on the
  psoas attachment — rules out #6-11, #16-17, #22 and #23.
- **Stretch under load:** the S-tier "face away" and "ab mat" versions (#5, #19,
  #27, #28, #30) get their stretch by bending the lower back backward under load
  — the movement the back-extension safety rule limits at L5/S1 (caution). The
  facing-the-machine versions (#26, #29) and the bench reverse crunch (#18) do
  not.
