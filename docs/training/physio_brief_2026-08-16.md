Hence the framing: **an endurance gap, not a volume gap.** This is the same

new region.

## 5. Four converging drivers

1. **The right shoulder loads the left side.** Post-Latarjet the right
   shoulder visibly sags and stability there is muscular, not ligamentous. The
   tell: the **July 2025 rhomboid strain was on the LEFT** despite the damaged
   shoulder being the right, and the 2025 log records a **left tilt** under
   overhead load. The left scapular stabilisers appear to be absorbing the
   postural cost of the right side. *This is the least obvious point here and
   the most decision-relevant.*
2. **Hypermobility (Beighton 6/9) makes sustained stillness the worst load
   case** — muscle fatigues, ligament does not. Same mechanism as the mid-back
   episodes, which flare after a day of sitting rather than after the activity,
   and as the 2026-07-06 left QL strain (isometric holding for a whole walk).
3. **Thoracic stiffness (T6–T10, sitting-driven, biomechanical finding #3)**
   tilts the scapula forward off the ribcage, leaving the retractors holding
   lengthened all day.
4. **Desk exposure with unsupported forearms** — the unchanged variable.

## 6. Already actioned, no sign-off required

- **Forearm support** — elbows were floating while typing, leaving the
  scapular retractors holding ~4 kg of arm weight per side all day. Both
  forearms now supported, elbows under shoulders, monitor top at eye level.
- **Movement break every 30–45 min, taken before onset** rather than after.
  The 20-minute walk was already proven to relieve it; it was simply mistimed.
- **No posture-chasing.** The 2026-07-06 entry records a deliberate postural
  correction producing a genuine strain in a different region, with the lesson
  written down at the time: *"a comfortable posture change is not a conditioned
  one."* Position is varied frequently rather than held corrected.
- **Release upper trap / levator before scapular activation**, mirroring the
  inhibit-then-activate sequencing already used for glute medius/piriformis.

## 7. Negatives and tripwires

**Absent:** arm symptoms, numbness, tingling, radiating pain, sharp pain,
acute onset, headache, dizziness, instability events.

**Escalate on:** any of the above appearing; or severity trending up from the
current flat 1–3/10; or no improvement following the section 6 changes.
